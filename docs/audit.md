# IEEE-CIS Data Audit

Generated by `python -m ml.audit`. Every downstream choice cites this file.

## Shape and balance

- Rows: **590,540**, columns: **434** (394 transaction + 41 identity, joined on `TransactionID`)
- Fraud: **20,663** rows = **3.50%** - imbalance ratio **1:28**
- Identity join coverage: **24.4%** of transactions carry identity data

PR-AUC is the headline metric. At this base rate a model that predicts 'never fraud' scores 96.5% accuracy, so accuracy is meaningless here.

## Time split

- `TransactionDT` is a **seconds offset**, not a unix timestamp: min=86,400 (day 1.0), max=15,811,131 (day 183.0)
- Span: **182 days**
- Chronological 60%/20%/20% split into train / validation / test
  - train ends at `TransactionDT = 8,745,782` (day 101.2)
  - validation ends at `TransactionDT = 12,192,854` (day 141.1)
- Fraud rate: train **3.38%**, validation **3.90%**, test **3.44%**

Split by time, never randomly. Card and address history bleeds across a random split and inflates every reported number.

Three sets, not two, and the reason is narrow: validation is spent on early stopping and on choosing the advisory thresholds, so anything measured on it has already been optimised against and reads high. Test is never looked at until the model and the thresholds are frozen. Every headline number in `docs/metrics.md` comes from test.

## Feature shortlist

**266 of 434 columns** survive, after removing the correlated, near-constant and >90%-null columns above.

Kept per block: `V*` 190/339, `C*` 13/14, `D*` 16/17, `M*` 8/9, `id_*` 27/38, `card*` 6/6, `addr*` 1/2, `dist*` 1/2.

Derived in `ml/features.py` (Phase 2), not here:

- `log_amount` (the raw amount spans 0.25-31,937, heavily right-skewed)
- `hour_of_day` and `day_of_week` from `TransactionDT`
- email domain grouping, since the provider long tail collapses to a few buckets
- frequency encoding for `card1`, `addr1`, `P_emaildomain`

`TransactionID` and `TransactionDT` are **excluded as model inputs**: the ID is an arbitrary counter and the raw offset would let the model memorise the split boundary. The derived hour and weekday are kept.
